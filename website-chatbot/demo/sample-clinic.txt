TEST PREPARATION
Fasting blood sugar and lipid profile tests require 8 to 10 hours of fasting. Water is allowed.
Ultrasound of the abdomen requires 6 hours of fasting. For a pelvic ultrasound, drink water and do not pass urine before the scan.
Lab reports are ready within 24 hours and can be collected from the reception or sent on WhatsApp.

APPOINTMENTS AND POLICIES
Appointments can be booked at the reception, by phone, or through the website chat.
Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment time.
To cancel or reschedule, inform the clinic at least 2 hours before your appointment.
Payment methods accepted: cash, Easypaisa, and JazzCash. Card payments are not accepted yet.
Home visit by a doctor is available within Mardan city for Rs 3,000, subject to doctor availability.
