Privacy Notice

This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use this document chatbot.

What is collected

  • The document you upload.
  • The questions you type.
  • Short excerpts from your document that match your question.

There is no account and no sign-up, so we do not collect your name or email through this app.

Why it is collected

Only to generate an answer for you. Your question and the matching excerpts from your document are used to write the reply you see, and to show you the sources for that reply.

Who processes it

  • Hosting: this app runs on Render, over a secure HTTPS connection.
  • Answer generation: your question and the matching excerpts are sent to the Groq API, which generates the answer text.

How long it is kept

Your uploaded document is kept on the server only until you delete it or the server restarts - then it is wiped. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Use the "Delete my document and chat data" button next to your active document. Clicking it removes your document collection from the server. Your document is also wiped automatically when the server restarts, so it will be gone the next time you load the page after a restart.

What stays on your own device

Your browser uses local storage (localStorage) to remember which document you uploaded, so the app can reconnect to it if you reload the page. If you enter your own Groq API key in Advanced settings, it is stored only in your browser on your device - it is not stored on the server as your key. You can clear both at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Cookies and tracking

This app does not use tracking or analytics cookies.

Please do not upload or type passwords, card numbers, or sensitive personal data. Only upload documents you are happy to have processed as described above.

Questions

Need a person? Talk to a human - this contacts the developer / support.
