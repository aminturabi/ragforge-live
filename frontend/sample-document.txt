Bright Cuts Hair Salon - Information

About us
Bright Cuts is a friendly hair salon at 42 Maple Street, Springfield.
You can call us on 555-0142 or email bookings@brightcuts.example

Opening hours
We are open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
We are closed on Sundays.

Services and prices
- Haircut: $25
- Kids haircut (under 12 years old): $15
- Hair colouring: $60
- Beard trim: $12

On Tuesdays, senior citizens get a 10% discount on all services.

How to book an appointment
To book an appointment, call us on 555-0142, email bookings@brightcuts.example,
or just walk in - walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free.
Booking ahead is recommended on Saturdays, our busiest day.

Cancelling or changing a booking
You can cancel or change your appointment free of charge up to 3 hours before it starts.
Just call us on 555-0142 and tell us your name and appointment time.

Good to know
We accept cash and card payments.
Free Wi-Fi is available in the salon while you wait.
